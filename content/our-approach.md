---
title: "Our Approach"
description: "How Top Acquisitions approaches conversations, diligence, transactions, handover, and long-term stewardship."
headline: "Calm process. Clear conversations. Long-term ownership."
summary: "Buying a business is not just a transaction. It is a transfer of responsibility. Our approach is deliberately careful because trust is built through how the process feels, not only through the final price."
steps:
  - title: "1. Discreet first conversation"
    body: "We start by understanding why an owner is considering change and whether we may be a suitable buyer."
  - title: "2. Understand the business"
    body: "We look at the customers, team, contracts, management accounts, owner dependency, systems, and practical operating risks."
  - title: "3. Agree a fair structure"
    body: "We aim for a structure that reflects the evidence, the founder's objectives, and the handover the business actually needs."
  - title: "4. Plan the transition"
    body: "A good handover protects staff confidence and customer service. We prefer thoughtful transition plans over rushed announcements."
  - title: "5. Steward the business"
    body: "After completion, the focus is continuity, better systems, steady reporting, and support for the team running the company."
note: "We work with patient capital partners where appropriate."
---
